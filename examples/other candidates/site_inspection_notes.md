# Site Inspection Notes (Exploratory Schema)

This stub represents a data schema for describing Site Inspection Notes created as a record of Building Control activity by Local Authority Building Control Bodies and Registered Building Control Approvers to meet the minimum statutory requirements under:

- Building Act 1984
- Building Regulations 2010 (as amended)
- Building (Registered Building Control Approvers etc.) (England) Regulations 2024

It set's out to record when inspections have taken place, what was inspected and any actions or outcomes requireing further attention.

This is not a statutory template. It is an exploratory discussion artefact.
