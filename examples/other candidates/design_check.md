# Design Check (Exploratory Schema)

This stub represents a data schema for describing a 'Design Check' (sometimes known as a 'Plan Check') as part of a Application for Building Regulation Approval (known as a Full Plans applucation) to meet the minimum statutory requirements under:

- Building Act 1984
- Building Regulations 2010 (as amended)
- Building (Registered Building Control Approvers etc.) (England) Regulations 2024

It set's out to record what plans have been checked, who has checked them and which parts of which approved documents are involved.

This is not a statutory template. It is an exploratory discussion artefact.
